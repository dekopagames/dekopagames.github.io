1Overview

Overblip is a mobile arcade game about tapping targets before they vanish, played in Competitive and Survival modes. This policy explains what information the game and the third-party services it relies on collect, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android version of Overblip distributed through Google Play.

Overblip is built for a general audience aged 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13.

2Information we collect

Most of your progress in Overblip — your player name, high scores, badges, equipped frame, and stats — is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us directly. Beyond that, three categories of information pass through services built into the game:

Play Games sign-in

Your Google Play Games account identifier, used to record and display achievement unlocks tied to your in-game badges.

Advertising

Advertising identifier, device and network information (including approximate location derived from your IP address) and ad interactions, collected by our ad partners to show and measure interstitial ads. Where required by law, we ask for your consent first.

Purchases

Purchase confirmation for cosmetic frames, handled entirely by Google Play Billing — we only ever see whether a purchase succeeded, never your payment details.

We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, or contacts, and Overblip does not have its own account or login system beyond the optional Play Games sign-in.

3Third-party services

Overblip is built with the following third-party SDKs, each governed by its own privacy policy in addition to this one:

  • Google Play Games Services — sign-in and achievements. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) ad mediation — chooses which ad network shows each interstitial ad and passes on your consent choice to them. See Unity's Player & App User Privacy Policy and the ironSource Privacy Policy.
  • Ad networks: ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob — provide the interstitial ads shown in Overblip. See the Unity and ironSource policies above, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — shows the privacy consent message described below and stores your choice. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases of cosmetic frames. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4Ads & your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Overblip shows a consent message from Google the first time you open the game, before any ads load. There you can agree to, refuse, or choose individually how our ad partners may use your information, for example whether ads may be personalized. Refusing does not stop you from playing: you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

You can change your choice at any time in the game under Options → Privacy settings.

Wherever you are, you can also reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads).

5How we use information

  • To operate core gameplay features, including achievements and cosmetic frame purchases.
  • To show and measure the interstitial ads that keep Overblip free to play.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical issues reported through the Google Play Console.

We do not use any of this information to build a profile of you outside of Overblip, and we do not sell your information.

6Data sharing

We share information only with the third-party services listed above (Google, Unity and ironSource), strictly so they can perform the functions Overblip uses them for (sign-in, ads, purchases), and for ads only as allowed by your consent choice where one applies. We do not otherwise share, rent, or sell information to third parties.

7Retention & your choices

Local gameplay data (scores, badges, equipped items) lives on your device and is deleted the moment you uninstall Overblip. Data held by Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, or our ad partners is retained according to their own policies, linked above — you can review or reset your ads personalization and Play Games activity from your Google account settings at any time.

8Children's privacy

Overblip is designed for a general audience aged 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will address it.

9Security

We rely on the security practices of Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, and our ad partners for any information that passes through them, and we do not store payment details ourselves at any point.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Overblip changes — for example, if new features or services are added. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of Overblip after a change means you accept the revised policy.
