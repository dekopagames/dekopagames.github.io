Tap fast. Hit precise. Score high.

A fast tap target game for Android. Targets keep appearing, and you hit them as close to the centre as you can, as quickly as you can. Simple to start, hard to master.

Three rounds, three ways to sweat

Round 1 · Still

Targets appear and stay put. Pure accuracy: nail the bullseye before they fade.

Round 2 · Moving

Targets drift and bounce off the frame. Faster targets are worth more.

Round 3 · Moving + shrinking

Now they shrink too. Wait for a smaller target and the bonus grows, but the clock is ticking.

Every tap stacks bonuses

AccuracyDead centre scores 100. The further out, the less you get.
SpeedHit a target right after it appears for up to double points.
DistanceSwing far across the screen between hits for up to ×3.
Movement & shrinkFaster and smaller targets multiply your score.
OverlapsTap where targets overlap to score them all, multiplied by how many you hit.
Streak & perfectionKeep hitting to grow your combo, and land 90%+ hits for a bonus that never resets.

Know your targets

Target

Lives for 5 seconds. Let one vanish and it can cost you.

Special target

Shows up every 15 seconds. The longer you wait, up to 10 seconds, the bigger the bonus.

Skull

Always on the move. Hit it and your points go negative. Don't.

Two modes

Competitive

Three rounds with short breaks between them, always exactly 3:00. Every run is directly comparable, so it's all about your best score.

Survival

No breaks and the board carries over. After 3:00 the game keeps going until 10 targets have slipped away. How long can you last?
