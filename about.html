Player by nature.

I'm here for the beautiful world of gaming.

Not just for the pixels, the victories, the high scores or the final boss. I'm here for the stories, the worlds, the characters, the impossible challenges, and those unforgettable moments that stay with you long after you put the controller down.

What I've collected isn't achievements on a profile, but lessons from the worlds I've visited.

I've laughed, cried, raged, explored, failed, retried, and occasionally questioned my life choices because of video games. But somewhere along the way, I realised something:

I didn't just want to play games. I wanted to create them.

So here I am, learning, experimenting and building projects of my own. Not because I think I can recreate the worlds that made me fall in love with gaming and the magic behind them, but because I'd love to give someone else a moment they'll remember.

Because every great game leaves something behind. And if one day someone feels even a fraction of what I felt playing the games that shaped me, I'll consider it a victory.

GG. And thanks for playing.
