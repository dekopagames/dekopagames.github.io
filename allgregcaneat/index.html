How to play

  1. Eat the dishesDishes appear on the tables. Stand next to one and hold E to eat it. The longer you eat, the more points you get.
  2. Read the menuEach dish gives a different amount of points per second, shown on the menu.
  3. Crack fortune cookiesCookies appear on the floor. Walk over one to crack it: you get a fortune message (nearly as important as in real life) and two LUCKY NUMBERS.
  4. Go for lucky dishesDishes with a lucky number give double points and light up green on the menu, until you open the next cookie.
  5. Mind the temperatureDishes go cold, so watch the bar under each one before it runs out.
  6. Fill the gas meterAll that food has to go somewhere. Eating fills Greg's gas meter, and when it's full Greg can unleash his... unique digestive power behind him. Anyone chasing him walks right into it, turns green and spins in confusion for a moment. The Chef recovers faster. He's smelled worse in his kitchen.

Controls

← ↑ ↓ →Move around.
E holdEat. Greg slows down while he's eating.
FRelease your secret weapon when the gas meter is full. Anyone right behind Greg suddenly loses interest in chasing.

Watch out

  • Greg has 3 lives. Getting caught costs one.
  • The waitresses see you eating from a distance. Hide behind tables and don't let them get you.
  • At closing time the Chef comes out (the horror). He never stops chasing Greg, and his ladle works like a boomerang.

Tips

  • Eat lucky dishes when nobody is looking.
  • Stop eating and run the moment you see a “?” or “!”.
  • Beat your high score. It's saved in your browser.
  • Press the fullscreen button (bottom right) for the best experience.
